### Overall Impression
Gymini is shaping up to be a production-grade, highly polished fitness application. The "Ink & Ember" identity is strong, the SQLite offline-first architecture is robust, and the recent UX iterations (adding undo, auto-saves, and fixing contrast) have elevated the usability significantly.

### What's Working
- **Identity**: The brutalist typography (`displayLg`, `headlineMd`) combined with dark surface colors creates a premium, tactile feel.
- **Architecture**: The client-side SQLite approach makes the app feel instantly responsive.
- **Micro-interactions**: Added touch targets and gesture-based interactions (long-press undo) make the app gym-ready.
